# Celestia'26 Event Cover Images Guide

Place all event cover image assets inside this folder: `public/images/events/`

## Recommended File Names
Use `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png`, or `.webp` extensions with lowercase event IDs or codes:

1. **Seraphia (Solo Singing)**: `seraphia.jpg` or `seraphia.png`
2. **Symphora (Instrumental Music)**: `symphora.jpg` or `symphora.png`
3. **Nrityara (Solo Classical Dance)**: `nrityara.jpg` or `nrityara.png`
4. **Reflexia (Replica Dance)**: `reflexia.jpg` or `reflexia.png`
5. **Stellar Bid (Mock IPL Auction)**: `stellar-bid.jpg` or `stellar_bid.jpg`
6. **Eclipse (Solo Western Dance)**: `eclipse.jpg` or `eclipse.png`
7. **Cosmic Switch (Adaptunes)**: `cosmic-switch.jpg` or `cosmic_switch.jpg`
8. **Twin Flare (Duo Dance)**: `twin-flare.jpg` or `twin_flare.jpg`
9. **Starfusion (Group Dance)**: `starfusion.jpg` or `starfusion.png`
10. **Elaris (Cosplay / Solo Fashion Walk)**: `elaris.jpg` or `elaris.png`
11. **Silhouette (Arabian Nights Fashion Parade)**: `silhouette.jpg` or `silhouette.png`
12. **AdBlitz (Adzap)**: `adblitz.jpg` or `adblitz.png`
13. **Mememania (Online Meme Competition)**: `mememania.jpg` or `mememania.png`
14. **Cineverse (Movie Recreation Reel)**: `cineverse.jpg` or `cineverse.png`

## How to upload on GitHub:
1. Go to your repository on GitHub.
2. Navigate to `public` > `images` > `events`.
3. Click **Add file** -> **Upload files**.
4. Drag and drop your image files with the exact names listed above.
5. Commit changes to the `main` branch.
6. Render will automatically redeploy with your custom images!
